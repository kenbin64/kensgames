It's all geometry — honestly

Everything you see and hear is generated from math. No images. No sound files.

The surface: the bugs ride an invisible Schwarz Diamond — a triply-periodic minimal surface, F(x,y,z)=0. Honest note: it's the nodal approximation, not the mathematically exact minimal surface.

The motion: each bug flows along that surface — its velocity is projected onto the surface and re-snapped to it — while it's drawn to the light. The camera gimbals along the same geometry.

The sights: the glow, sparks, lightning arcs and colour are all procedural — drawn, not loaded.

The sound: every sound is synthesized live — the electric hum, the zap crackle, the crickets, the fly-bys — and the ambient music's notes are sampled from the manifold itself (the surface height picks the pitch). "From the manifold" means exactly that, nothing mystical.

A relaxing toy, and a small demo of the idea — not a product. It claims nothing beyond being made of geometry.

🪲 No bugs were harmed in the making of this program — except maybe the ones that flew into a wind-farm turbine or through a solar array. Those were real, and yes, they're dead. Not the ones in here.

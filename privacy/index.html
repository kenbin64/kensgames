Privacy Policy

KensGames · Last updated: June 15, 2026

KensGames is run by Kenneth Bingham. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what data we collect, why, and how you can remove it. The short version: you can play solo without an account, we never sell your data, and you can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Playing without an account

Solo play (against the computer) does not require an account. A display name and avatar you choose are stored locally in your browser or app and are not sent to us as an account. You only need to sign in for online multiplayer and saved stats.

What we collect when you sign in

If you sign in with Google: we receive your email address, your name, and your Google profile picture. We do not receive your Google password and have no access to your Google account beyond those basics.

If you sign in with email and password: we store your email and a one-way bcrypt hash of your password. We never store your actual password and cannot recover it — only reset it.

We also store, tied to your account:

  • your chosen display name and avatar;
  • game results and stats (wins, games played) for online play;
  • basic technical logs (e.g. timestamps, error logs) needed to run the service and prevent abuse.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not store your payment details (the games are free).
  • We do not track you across other websites for advertising.

Advertising

The games are free. If we introduce ads in the future (for example Google AdSense), this policy will be updated first to describe what those partners collect, and you will be told before any ads appear.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage and a session token to keep you signed in and to remember your display name and avatar. These are functional, not advertising trackers.

How your data is stored

Account data is stored on our own server. Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. We keep your data only as long as your account exists.

Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time by emailing ken.bingham64@gmail.com. We will remove your account data promptly.

Children

KensGames is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email ken.bingham64@gmail.com.

FastTrack rules and help

Race your five pegs around the board and home. Send your friends back to the start on the way.

1. Quick start

  1. Draw a card. Tap the deck (or 🃏 Draw).
  2. Pick a peg. Your pegs that can move show numbers. Press the same number at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Pick where it lands, if there is a choice. The holes show letters. Press the letter, or tap the lit hole.
  4. Press ✓ to play it. The peg hops there.
  5. Got an Ace, 6, Jack, Queen, King or Joker? You draw again.

If a card gives you only one possible play, the game plays it for you. If it gives you none, your turn ends.

Three things to remember: an Ace, a 6 or a Joker brings a peg out, landing exactly on another player sends their peg back, and you can never land on or pass your own peg.

2. The goal top ↑

You have five pegs. Take them clockwise around the board and bring them home. Four go into your safe zone. The fifth finishes on your home hole. The first player to do it wins.

Two to six can play: people, bots, or both.

3. The board top ↑

The board has six color areas, one for each player. The track runs clockwise through all six. Here is one color area, hole by hole.

PlaceWhat it is
Holding areaWhere pegs wait to come out. Four of your pegs start here. Pegs here cannot be hit.
Home holeWhere a peg comes out, and where your last peg must finish to win. One of your pegs starts here.
Fast track holesSix of them, one at the start of each color area, in a ring around the middle. They are the shortcut.
BullseyeThe one hole in the very middle. See the bullseye.
Safe zoneYour four holes leading in from your entrance. Only your pegs go there, and they cannot be hit. See the safe zone.

4. Your turn top ↑

  1. Draw one card. Each player has their own full deck of 54 cards: 52 and two Jokers. When your deck runs out, your used cards are shuffled into a new deck.
  2. Move, if you can. If the card gives you a legal move, you must take one. You may not skip a turn.
  3. No legal move? The card is used up and your turn ends. (A play-again card still lets you draw again.)
  4. Play-again card? Draw another card. Otherwise the turn passes clockwise.

Who goes first: a random player in the first game. After Play again, the winner of the last game goes first.

You must use the whole count. A 5 is exactly 5 hops, never 4 and never 6. The only card you can share between pegs is the 7.

The one-hop cards are the Ace, Jack, Queen, King and Joker. They matter for the bullseye.

6. Play-again cards top ↑

Six cards give you another draw as soon as you have played them:

A 6 J Q K 🃏

You draw again even when the card gave you no legal move. There is no limit: draw three in a row and you play three times.

7. Getting out of the holding area top ↑

Only three cards bring a peg out: A 6 🃏

  1. The peg goes from holding onto your home hole. That is the whole move: it does not also hop.
  2. All three are play-again cards, so you draw again straight away.
  • Your own peg on your home hole? You cannot bring another one out until it moves off.
  • Another player's peg on your home hole? You can still come out. Their peg is sent back.
  • A peg out and a peg waiting? With one of these cards you choose: New peg brings one out, Board peg moves a peg that is already out.

8. Moving, and your own pegs top ↑

  • Pegs move clockwise, one hole per hop. Only the 4 goes backward.
  • The count must be exact.
  • You can pass over other players' pegs. Nothing happens to them.

You can never land on your own peg, and you can never pass your own peg. Not on the main track, not on the fast track, not in the safe zone. If your own peg is in the way, that peg cannot make the move.

On the fast track this goes one step further. A fast track hop jumps a whole color area, so your own peg anywhere in the area you would jump blocks the hop, and so does your own peg on the next fast track hole.

One exception: a peg in the bullseye never blocks anyone. It is in the middle, off the track.

9. Landing on opponents top ↑

If your move ends exactly on another player's peg, you take the hole and their peg is sent back. The game calls this a cut.

  • The cut peg goes to its holding area. If its holding area is already full, it goes to its home hole.
  • Only the last hop counts. Passing over a peg does nothing.

10. The fast track top ↑

The six fast track holes make a ring around the middle of the board. A peg riding the ring hops from one fast track hole straight to the next. One hop on the ring covers a whole color area: 14 holes of main track.

Getting on

Land exactly on any fast track hole, moving clockwise. From your home hole, a 5 lands on the next fast track hole. The game shows FASTTRACK!

Riding it

  1. On your next cards, that peg hops around the ring, one fast track hole per hop.
  2. The ride ends at your own fast track hole. Any hops left over carry on down the main track toward your safe zone.
  3. Reaching your own fast track hole counts as having gone around the board.

Example: your peg is five ring hops from your own fast track hole and you draw a 10. It rides 5 hops to your hole, then walks 5 more. That is 75 holes of main track covered with one card.

Getting off early, and losing the ride

  • While a peg is riding, the game offers only moves that keep it riding. 🪂↪ (bail out) lets it get off early and walk the main track instead. That ends its ride.
  • Moving a different peg also ends the ride. The peg stays on its hole, but from then on it walks the main track.
  • Drawing a 4 ends the ride for every peg you have on the fast track, whichever peg you move.
  • Your own pegs block the ring like anywhere else.
  • Backing onto a fast track hole with a 4 does not get you on. You only get on moving clockwise.

11. The bullseye top ↑

The bullseye is the single hole in the very middle. It holds one peg. From it, a peg can come out on any fast track hole, including your own, which is the fastest way home in the game.

Getting in

There are two ways.

  1. From the fast track. A peg riding the fast track can hop into the bullseye with any one-hop card: A J Q K 🃏. Press 🎯.
  2. From the main track. If your count reaches a fast track hole (any except your own) with exactly one hop left, that last hop can go into the bullseye. From your home hole a 6 does it: 5 hops to the fast track hole, 1 into the bullseye.
  • You cannot enter going backward with a 4.
  • You cannot enter if your own peg is already there.
  • If another player's peg is there, you take the bullseye and their peg is sent back.

Getting out

Only a Jack, Queen or King gets a peg out. Not an Ace, not a Joker, not a number card.

  • The peg comes out on any fast track hole that does not hold your own peg. Leave 🎯 goes to your own fast track hole. Pick 🏁 lets you choose another.
  • If another player's peg is on that hole, it is sent back.
  • Coming out on your own fast track hole counts as having gone around the board. Your safe zone entrance is 7 hops ahead.
  • A peg that comes out is not riding the fast track. It walks the main track from there.

A peg in the bullseye is stuck until you draw a Jack, Queen or King, and it can be cut by anyone who enters. There are 12 of those cards in your 54.

12. The safe zone top ↑

Your safe zone is four holes leading in from your safe zone entrance. Nothing can hit a peg in there.

  1. Go around first. A peg can turn in only after it has gone around: it has reached or passed your own fast track hole. (There is one shortcut: the 4.)
  2. Turn in at the entrance. The hops carry on into the zone: from the entrance, 1 hop is the first safe hole and 4 hops is the last.
  3. The count must fit. A peg that has gone around cannot walk past the entrance. If the card is too big for the holes that are left, that peg cannot use it.
  • Only your own pegs can enter your safe zone.
  • Inside, pegs move forward only, by exact count. A 4 cannot move a peg in the safe zone.
  • You cannot pass your own peg in there either, so the zone fills from the far end.

13. Winning top ↑

  1. Get four pegs into your safe zone, filling all four holes.
  2. Bring your fifth peg around the board.
  3. Land it exactly on your home hole. From your safe zone entrance, that is 2 hops.

The count must be exact. One too many is not a legal move. One short is fine: the peg stops a hole away and you need a one-hop card next time.

When your safe zone is full and your last peg is on the way in, a 👑 crown appears over your home hole. Everyone can see you are one landing from winning.

14. The 7 top ↑

A 7 can be played two ways.

  1. One peg moves all 7.
  2. Split it between two pegs. The first peg moves 1 to 6 hops. The second peg moves the rest, so the two add up to 7.
  • Both pegs must already be out. A peg in holding cannot take part.
  • The parts are played in order: the peg you pick first moves first, then the second moves on the board as it now stands.
  • Each part is a normal move. It can cut, land on a fast track hole, enter the bullseye or step into the safe zone, if that would be legal on its own.
  • If you have only one peg out, it must take all 7.

On screen: pick the first peg, then its stopping hole. Stops are labelled by route and count, so A3 means stop after 3 hops on route A. Then pick the peg that takes the rest.

15. The 4 top ↑

The 4 is the only card that moves backward: 4 hops against the clock.

  • It can cut like any other move.
  • It cannot back into the bullseye or the safe zone, and it cannot move a peg that is already in the safe zone.
  • It can land on a fast track hole, but that does not put the peg on the fast track.
  • It ends the ride of every peg you have on the fast track.
  • It cannot back over your own peg.

The shortcut. Backing onto or past your own safe zone entrance counts as having gone around the board. A 4 from your home hole, or from one or two holes past it, does this. Your next forward card can then turn straight into your safe zone. See strategy.

17. Playing with friends top ↑

  1. On the FastTrack front page choose Play with friends, type your name, and press Start a table.
  2. Send your friends the five-letter code or the invite link. Each plays on their own phone or computer.
  3. Everyone picks a name, a piece and a color. One player per color.
  4. The host adds bots for any empty seats, then presses Start the game.
  • No sign-up. Nobody registers anything.
  • No time limit, unless the host ticks ⏱ Turn time limit before starting. With it off, nobody is timed and no bot ever takes a person's seat. With it on, a player idle for 2 minutes gets a 30-second warning, and after that the host may hand their turns to a bot.
  • Keep the game's tab open. If the page drops or freezes, reload it in that same tab and you are back in your seat.
  • Play again: the host starts the next game. Every peg goes back to the start, every deck is shuffled, everyone keeps their color, and the winner goes first.

18. Strategy top ↑

These are tips, not rules.

Get pegs out

Only 10 of your 54 cards bring a peg out: four Aces, four 6s and two Jokers. When you draw one, a new peg is usually the better play. More pegs out means fewer wasted cards.

Count to the fast track

From your home hole the next fast track hole is 5 hops away. A 5 puts you on it. A 6 can go one better and land in the bullseye.

Use the bullseye as a slingshot

A Jack, Queen or King takes a bullseye peg to your own fast track hole, with your safe zone 7 hops ahead. But the peg waits until one of those 12 cards turns up, and it can be cut while it waits. Take the bullseye when you can afford to wait.

The 4 from home

A peg on your home hole, or one or two holes past it, can back over your safe zone entrance with a 4. That counts as going around. From your home hole, a 4 followed by a 3 puts that peg in your first safe hole, without a lap of the board.

Do not block yourself

Your own pegs are walls. Two pegs close together on the same stretch can leave you with no legal move, and a peg sitting on a fast track hole stops your other pegs hopping the ring past it. Keep them spread out.

Fill the safe zone from the back

A peg in the first safe hole blocks everything behind it, because pegs cannot pass each other in there. Send the first pegs deep. Low cards are the ones that finish, so do not waste them early.

Watch the count behind you

A peg can be cut on the main track, on your home hole, on a fast track hole and in the bullseye. It is safe only in holding and in the safe zone. A peg one to ten holes in front of an opponent is in range of a single card.

Watch for the crown

When a 👑 appears over someone's home hole, they need one exact landing to win. Cutting that last peg sends it all the way back.

Copyright & Takedown Requests

KensGames · Last updated: June 15, 2026

KensGames respects intellectual property rights. Our titles are original works or original 3D reinterpretations, released under their own names. If you are a rights holder (or their authorized agent) and believe a KensGames title infringes your copyright or trademark, we want to hear from you directly.

Our promise: upon receipt of a genuine request that we can verify comes from the rights holder or an authorized agent, the title in question will be taken down immediately while we review it. We would rather remove a game and talk than leave a good-faith concern unanswered.

How to send a takedown / infringement notice

Email ken.bingham64@gmail.com with the subject line "Copyright/Trademark Notice" and include:

  1. Identification of the work or trademark you own (title, registration number if any, or a link).
  2. Identification of the KensGames title and page/URL you believe infringes it.
  3. Your name, organization, and contact information.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by you, the law, or your agent.
  5. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and that you are the rights holder or authorized to act on their behalf.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

These are the standard elements of a copyright notice (17 U.S.C. §512(c)(3)). Including them helps us verify your request quickly so we can act on it.

Verification

To prevent abuse (false or competitive takedowns), we verify that a request genuinely comes from the rights holder or their authorized agent before treating it as final. Verification will not delay an interim takedown of the title while we review.

Counter-process

If a title is removed and you believe the removal was a mistake, contact us at the same address and we will work with you and the original requester in good faith.
